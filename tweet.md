I just built "Minesweeper Succinct" for the Succinct Rockstars: Week 2 competition! 🕵️‍♂️💣 #SuccinctRockStars

This classic game features a cryptographic twist using SP1 zk Proofs to verify scores without revealing your strategy.

Added a unique "Cash Out" feature where players can secure partial scores after revealing 30% of the board, avoiding the risk of hitting mines.

Technical highlights:
• WASM for core game logic & performance
• SP1 for generating verifiable ZK proofs
• Local proof verification in the browser
• Detailed educational content about ZK proofs
