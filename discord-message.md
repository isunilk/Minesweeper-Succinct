**What makes it special:**
- Classic Minesweeper gameplay with a ZK twist
- Uses SP1 to generate verifiable proofs of your scores
- Unique "Cash Out" feature to secure partial scores (30%+ progress)
- Local proof verification right in your browser

I built this to showcase how ZK proofs can enhance gaming experiences while keeping things fun and educational. The About page explains how SP1 works for those new to ZK proofs.
